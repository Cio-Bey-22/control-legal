Privacy Policy - CONTROL

CONTROL processes only the minimum data required to provide bot functionality.

  • Data may include server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, user IDs, command usage, moderation/log events, and ticket metadata.
  • Message content is processed only when necessary for moderation or command handling.
  • Data is used solely for bot operation, security, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • Data may be stored temporarily or persistently as needed for features.
  • Server admins or users may request data deletion where applicable.
  • Discord and third-party services may process data under their own privacy policies.
